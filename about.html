02 / hello

hi, I'm Lavannya

I'm 19, and I'm in med school. Right now that looks like an alarm I lose to every morning, notes in three different handwritings, and the same diagram redrawn four times until the fourth one finally made sense.

The rest of me is less organised. I read well past the hour I promised myself I'd sleep. I photograph the sky like it's going to run out. There's a note on my phone that's nothing but lines from books I didn't want to lose.

No five-year plan, no tidy summary. I'm 19 and permanently interested in something, and so far that's been enough to go on.
