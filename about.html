02 / hello

hi, I'm Lavannya

I'm 19, and I'm in med school — which right now looks like an alarm I lose to every morning and the same diagram redrawn four times until the fourth one made sense.

The rest of me is less organised. I read past the hour I promised myself I'd sleep, and I photograph the sky like it's going to run out. No five-year plan yet, and so far that's been fine.
