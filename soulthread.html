soulthread

thread · noun the quiet line running between you and everything else. you cannot see it and you cannot cut it, and it pulls gently whenever you drift too far.

i feel a string i cannot see, it ties the stars back into me. it’s quiet, soft, and always near, it pulls me close when i feel fear.

it’s not a rope, it doesn’t hurt, just makes me feel i’m part of earth. it reminds me, when i forget, i still have light inside me yet.

the softest bond is often the strongest. lavannya daigavane
